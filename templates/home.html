DEMO PROJECT · ADMISSIONS OFFICE

An assistant that only says what's on file.

This is a retrieval-grounded FAQ assistant built for a fictional university's admissions office. It answers deadline, fee, and requirement questions from a fixed knowledge base — never inventing a date or a dollar figure — while still being able to hold a normal conversation around the edges: greetings, thanks, "what can you help with." HOW IT WORKS
01

Match the question

Your question is scored against every knowledge base entry using a blend of TF-IDF similarity and keyword overlap against curated paraphrases — tuned to avoid both generic-word over-matching and short-query misses.

02

Ground the reply

If the match clears the confidence threshold, the matched fact — and only that fact — is handed to Grok with instructions to phrase it naturally without adding anything. That's what keeps answers accurate instead of just plausible.

03

Talk, or forward

No confident match doesn't mean a dead end: small talk and general questions get a normal conversational reply. A genuine factual question with no match is forwarded to the admissions office instead of being guessed at.
